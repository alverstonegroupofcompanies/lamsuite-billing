# Impact Analysis — Counter-only POS (Windows till), Salespeople, Approvals, Pharmacist rule

**Change request date:** 2026-11-19
**Baseline:** `main` @ `a87cc0d` (v2.1.1)
**Status:** DRAFT — no application code written. Awaiting owner approval.
**Siblings:** [spec-delta.md](spec-delta.md) · [plan-delta.md](plan-delta.md) · [task-delta.md](task-delta.md)

## 1. What the CR asks for, in one paragraph

The till stops being a place where people log in. A Windows POS machine claims a **pre-created Counter** with a **one-time activation code**; the counter (not a human) is the identity, the permission boundary and the audit subject; the counter carries a **server-enforced permission profile**, its own **unlock PIN** and **number series**. Humans at the counter become two new kinds of record: **salespeople** (no login, chosen on the bill, optionally with a personal PIN, optionally flagged **pharmacist** with a registration number and validity dates) and **approvers** (store managers/owners with personal PINs) who sign off **sensitive actions** through PIN-at-counter, one-time PIN, or remote approval. In pharmacy stores, any bill containing a **restricted drug** requires an identified pharmacist's PIN before it can be saved, returned or cancelled. All of it is store-scoped and applies to every store type except the pharmacist rule, which is pharmacy-only.

## 3. Headline

**This is not a greenfield build and it is not a small change.** Roughly 45 % of Part 1 and Part 3 already exists in a partially-built, partly-insecure form; Part 2's enforcement layer, Part 4 and Part 5 are essentially greenfield. The single largest risk is not any new feature — it is **deleting the staff-login path that today carries shift cash, session audit, approvals and offline credentials**, and replacing the **unauthenticated public counter endpoints** that are currently the product's worst live security hole.

| CR part | Existing today | Must be built | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 Counters & registration | ~60 % | counter-precreated-then-claimed flow, per-counter codes, one-active-device rule, migration, removal of owner-login/store-key | **Rework + hardening** |
| P2 Profile & locking | ~10 % (data fields exist, enforcement does not) | full server-side policy engine, profile propagation, counter PIN | **Mostly greenfield** |
| P3 Approvals | ~50 % (`invoice_approvals` is a good chassis) | action widening, approver list, counter binding, inbox, alerts, server-side enforcement | **Extend + enforce** |

## 4. Current state in detail

### 4.1 Part 1 — Counters and registration

Already exists (and can be kept):

- A real `counters` table with `store_id`, `counter_code` (unique per store), `counter_name`, `device_name`, `location_name`, `status` (`pending_approval|active|locked|revoked`), `device_uuid` (unique), `pairing_code`/`pairing_expires_at`, `device_token`/`device_token_hash`, `approved_at/approved_by`, `role_profile`, `allowed_user_ids`, `visible_menus`, `printer_profile`, `receipt_customization`, `manager_pin`, `last_seen_at`, `last_sync_at`, `last_synced_watermark`, `revoke_reason` — [create_counters_and_remote_sync_tables.php:13-39](../../../backend/database/migrations/2026_10_18_233917_create_counters_and_remote_sync_tables.php#L13), [Counter.php:9-174](../../../backend/app/Models/Counter.php#L9), plus [add_iam_fields_to_counters_and_users.php:12-25](../../../backend/database/migrations/2026_10_23_173302_add_iam_fields_to_counters_and_users.php#L12) and the SQLite-only partial unique live-name index at [add_unique_active_counter_name.php:43-47](../../../backend/database/migrations/2026_11_14_000005_add_unique_active_counter_name.php#L43).
- Per-counter **number series** with block leasing: `counter_sequences` (`counter_id`, `doc_type`, `financial_year`, `prefix`, `range_start/range_end`, `next_number`, unique `(counter_id,doc_type,financial_year)`) — [same migration:44-57](../../../backend/database/migrations/2026_10_18_233917_create_counters_and_remote_sync_tables.php#L44), leased by [CounterController::leaseSequence:266-334](../../../backend/app/Http/Controllers/Api/CounterController.php#L266) and used offline by [counterSequenceService.ts](../../../frontend/src/lib/auth/counterSequenceService.ts).
- **Shifts/day-close** per counter: `counter_shifts`, `store_day_closes` ([same migration:61-102](../../../backend/database/migrations/2026_10_18_233917_create_counters_and_remote_sync_tables.php#L61)), `openShift`/`closeShift`/`closeStoreDay` ([CounterController.php:339,397,472](../../../backend/app/Http/Controllers/Api/CounterController.php#L339)).
- Hashed **activation keys** with expiry, single-use, device caps and brute-force lockout: `activation_keys` + `device_activation_attempts` ([create_activation_keys_and_device_tokens.php:11-58](../../../backend/database/migrations/2026_10_26_113000_create_activation_keys_and_device_tokens.php#L11)), [ActivationKey.php:55-143](../../../backend/app/Models/ActivationKey.php#L55), 5-failures/15-min lockout at [AuthController.php:58-79](../../../backend/app/Http/Controllers/Api/AuthController.php#L58).
- Device-token middleware that already forces tenant from the token: [AuthenticateDevice.php:12-100](../../../backend/app/Http/Middleware/AuthenticateDevice.php#L12).
- Multi-store isolation on one machine already works: per-store SQLite files, per-store vault keys `store:{store_id}:device_token`, store gateway/switcher ([SCREENS_PERMISSIONS_MATRIX.md:104-115](../../../SCREENS_PERMISSIONS_MATRIX.md#L104)).

Contradicts the CR and must change:

1. **Counters are auto-created by the till, never pre-created by the owner.** [CounterController::enroll:124-147](../../../backend/app/Http/Controllers/Api/CounterController.php#L124) creates the row when the device asks. There is no admin-panel create/edit/delete for counters at all — [DevicesPage.tsx](../../../admin-panel/src/pages/DevicesPage.tsx) only renames/locks/unlocks/revokes and edits four `role_profile` flags (lines 234, 251, 266-269, 279).
2. **Codes are per-store, not per-counter.** `activation_keys` has no `counter_id`; `generateKey()` also writes plaintext `stores.activation_key`, which is `$fillable` and **not hidden** on [Store.php:12-32](../../../backend/app/Models/Store.php#L12) — the shared store key is therefore returned by `GET /api/admin/stores`. [AuthController.php:86-100](../../../backend/app/Http/Controllers/Api/AuthController.php#L86) still accepts that legacy plaintext key and silently self-migrates it.
3. **Pairing codes are 6 characters (`XX-####`, 15 min), not single-use, and are redeemed through a route that requires no authentication.** `POST /api/counters/approve-pairing` is public ([api.php:69](../../../backend/routes/api.php#L69)), matched only by the code ([CounterController.php:543-599](../../../backend/app/Http/Controllers/Api/CounterController.php#L543)) and it **returns the plaintext device token**. `GET /counters/enrollment-status/{device_uuid}` ([:507-538](../../../backend/app/Http/Controllers/Api/CounterController.php#L507)) and `POST /counters/{id}/status` ([:184-227](../../../backend/app/Http/Controllers/Api/CounterController.php#L184), public per [api.php:167](../../../backend/routes/api.php#L167)) leak/escalate the same way: any caller can flip a counter to `active` and collect a token. This matches the QA finding "the public API block is the largest attack surface" ([test-reports/summary.md](../../../test-reports/summary.md), risk 4) and defect group G6.
4. **Owner login exists as a first-class registration path** — `POST /api/devices/register-owner` ([api.php:65](../../../backend/routes/api.php#L65), [AuthController.php:269-426](../../../backend/app/Http/Controllers/Api/AuthController.php#L269)) with email+password+TOTP, and a "Store Key / Owner Login" toggle in the desktop setup screen ([StoreSetupPage.tsx:25,241-264](../../../frontend/src/pages/StoreSetupPage.tsx#L25), [storeDeviceService.ts:306-443](../../../frontend/src/lib/auth/storeDeviceService.ts#L306)). The CR removes both.
5. **A counter can be claimed by many devices.** Nothing enforces "one active device per counter": `registerDevice` upserts `devices` by `device_uuid` and re-resolves a counter for each new UUID; the only uniqueness is `device_uuid`.
6. Tokens are stored **both plaintext and hashed** (`devices.device_token`, `counters.device_token`), so a DB dump is a token dump. The CR's "per-device token in OS secure storage" needs the server side to become hash-only.
7. **The "OS secure storage" is not OS secure storage.** The Tauri `secure_store_*` commands persist a JSON blob at `.lamsuite_secure_vault.dat` in the app directory ([frontend/src-tauri/src/main.rs:694-733](../../../frontend/src-tauri/src/main.rs#L694)). Only the offline staff-hash key is genuinely in the OS keychain path ([offlineStaffVault.ts:1-53](../../../frontend/src/lib/auth/offlineStaffVault.ts#L1)). See open question Q2.
8. **Counter names are invented by the device.** `registerDevice` derives `counter_code` from digits typed in the device name ([AuthController.php:156-157](../../../backend/app/Http/Controllers/Api/AuthController.php#L156)) — under the CR the code and name come from the owner-created counter, so this derivation must go.
9. Number-series plumbing is inconsistent: backend doc types are `invoice|sales_return|receipt|stock_transfer` ([CounterController.php:270,676-698](../../../backend/app/Http/Controllers/Api/CounterController.php#L676) seeds only three), while the till's local map uses `INV/SR/REC/TRF` with different keys, never calls `leaseSequence`, defaults to `next_number 1001` when a row is missing, and falls back to `TEMP-C01-XXXX`; the online path calls `/api/counters/{counterCode}/lease-sequence` where the route binds a numeric PK ([onlineTransactionService.ts:276-351](../../../frontend/src/lib/sync/onlineTransactionService.ts#L276)) — a latent defect that must be fixed while counter series become first-class.
10. **Offline grace / revoked-locks-offline is unimplemented.** Today revocation only bites on the next handshake ([AuthController::validateToken:431-519](../../../backend/app/Http/Controllers/Api/AuthController.php#L431) → `lock_and_reset`), with no owner-settable offline grace period and no local counter-status expiry.

### 4.2 Part 2 — Permission profile and locking

- The **data fields exist but nothing reads them server-side.** `counters.role_profile` is written once at enroll as four booleans (`allow_cancel, allow_price_change, allow_stock_adj, require_manager_pin`) ([CounterController.php:136-141](../../../backend/app/Http/Controllers/Api/CounterController.php#L136)) and the only consumer is [storeDeviceService.canPerformAction:890-899](../../../frontend/src/lib/auth/storeDeviceService.ts#L890) — a function **no code in `frontend/src` imports**. `visible_menus`, `receipt_customization`, `allowed_user_ids` are writable through `POST /admin/counters/{id}/settings` ([api.php:309](../../../backend/routes/api.php#L309)) and consumed nowhere except `allowed_user_ids` in staff login ([StaffAuthController.php:166-177](../../../backend/app/Http/Controllers/Api/StaffAuthController.php#L166)).
- `counters.manager_pin` (DDL line 27) is in `$hidden` and is **never written or read anywhere** — the CR's counter unlock PIN does not exist.
- Screen hiding today is **role-based**, not counter-based: [accessControl.ts](../../../frontend/src/lib/auth/accessControl.ts), [reports/permissions.ts](../../../frontend/src/reports/permissions.ts), per-user `enabled_menus` / `staff_users.enabled_menus_json`, store-level `enabled_menu_items` (CFG-002). Under decision 2 ("roles exist only for admin panel users") all POS-side role gating must be re-keyed to the counter profile.
- No server-side discount cap exists. The only threshold-style policy in the codebase is role-based stock-adjustment approval ([app/Services/StockAdjustmentPolicy.php:225-237](../../../backend/app/Services/StockAdjustmentPolicy.php#L225)) — a good pattern to copy, not a substitute.
- Idle lock exists but is **per staff session**: 5-minute default in [staffAuthService.ts:113-116,354](../../../frontend/src/lib/auth/staffAuthService.ts#L113), timer in [Layout.tsx:195-217](../../../frontend/src/components/Layout.tsx#L195), lock screen [LockOverlay.tsx](../../../frontend/src/components/LockOverlay.tsx) unlocking a *user* session. Shift open/close today takes `$request->user()?->id` and, being on a public route, is frequently null ([CounterController.php:339-467](../../../backend/app/Http/Controllers/Api/CounterController.php#L339)).

### 4.3 Part 3 — Approvals

Good news: the chassis is already the right shape. `invoice_approvals` carries `store_id`, `invoice_id`, `action` (`edit|cancel`), `mode` (`manager|one_time_pin`), `status` (`pending|approved|used|revoked|expired`), `requested_by_user_id/name/counter`, `approved_by_user_id/name`, `pin_hash`, `token_hash`, `reason`, `expires_at`, `used_at`, `meta` ([migration:11-38](../../../backend/database/migrations/2026_11_14_000003_create_invoice_approvals_table.php#L11)). One-time PINs are 6-digit, hashed, TTL 10 min (max 60), shown once, bound to invoice+action+target user, single-use ([InvoiceApprovalController.php:72-140,284-360](../../../backend/app/Http/Controllers/Api/InvoiceApprovalController.php#L72)); manager mode checks `password` **or** `pin_hash`, applies lockout after `max_failed_logins`, audits `invoice_approval_issued|used|failed|revoked`.

Gaps: approvals cover **only invoice edit and cancel**; the granting token is honoured **only by the client** ([invoiceApprovalService.ts:78-91,107](../../../frontend/src/lib/billing/invoiceApprovalService.ts#L78) stores a localStorage grant; [PosBillingPage.tsx:2895-2902](../../../frontend/src/pages/PosBillingPage.tsx#L2895) reads it back). `InvoiceController::cancel` ([:1034](../../../backend/app/Http/Controllers/Api/InvoiceController.php#L1034)) and `SyncController` contain **zero** approval verification, so a direct API call bypasses the entire control. The approval dialog is reachable only from Past Bills ([InvoiceApprovalDialog.tsx](../../../frontend/src/components/InvoiceApprovalDialog.tsx), imported at [BillsPage.tsx:57,2152](../../../frontend/src/pages/BillsPage.tsx#L57)), never from the billing screen. No `counter_id` on the approval row, no remote-approval inbox UI, no anomaly alerts, no discount/price-override/return/stock-adjustment/purchase/large-refund/sell-beyond-stock rules.

### 4.4 Part 4 — Salespeople

Nothing exists. `invoices.user_id` is a nullable FK ([create_transactions_tables.php:15](../../../backend/database/migrations/2026_10_01_000003_create_transactions_tables.php#L15)) that **no code path ever writes** — `InvoiceController::store` and `SyncController::upsertSingleInvoice` ([:242](../../../backend/app/Http/Controllers/Api/SyncController.php#L242)) ignore it, and the local `invoices` table has no operator column at all ([sqliteStorage.ts:21-53](../../../frontend/src/lib/db/sqliteStorage.ts#L21)). ## 5. Blast radius

**Data layer** — 3 new tables (`salespeople`, `approvals` renamed/ widened from `invoice_approvals`, `pharmacist_confirmations`), 2 new lookup-ish tables (`permission_profile_templates`, `drug_schedules` — or `master_lookups.type='drug_schedule'`), and column additions on `counters` (permission profile JSON + version, unlock PIN hash/lockout, grace hours, idle minutes, current device), `activation_keys` (`counter_id`, single-use hardening), `products` (schedule + restricted + prescription-required), `invoices`/`invoice_items` (salesperson id/name snapshot, per-line salesperson, pharmacist id/name/reg/confirmed_at), `stores` (salesperson mode config, restricted-drug config). Local SQLite gains schema version 9 (current last migration is v8, versions are non-contiguous — [sqliteStorage.ts:9,636-658](../../../frontend/src/lib/db/sqliteStorage.ts#L9)). Deletions: `users` POS-role rows are converted, not deleted; plaintext `stores.activation_key` is dropped. See [plan-delta.md §2](plan-delta.md).

**API** — roughly 40 new routes across ~15 endpoint groups; 6 retired (`/devices/register-owner`, `/devices/register`, public `/counters/enroll`, public `/counters/approve-pairing`, public `/counters/{id}/status`, `/staff/login` at the till) plus `/staff/offline-cache` reshaped, and **every existing device-token write endpoint gains a profile/approval/pharmacist gate**. [openapi.yaml](../../api/openapi.yaml) is 405 lines / 15 paths and documents only `/devices/register` of the whole device-counter-staff-approval family, so the contract starts well behind the implementation.

**POS client** — setup screen becomes code-only; staff login screen, account switcher (`F12`), lock overlay, shift open/close, cashier-keyed cash drawer (`cash_shifts.user_id`, [sqliteStorage.ts:425](../../../frontend/src/lib/db/sqliteStorage.ts#L425)) and `counter_sessions` all change owner; billing header gains a type-ahead + shortcut; the hint bar, `centralKeyMap` and the published [specs/keyboard-shortcuts.json](../../keyboard-shortcuts.json) (71 entries, asserted equal by [publishedKeyMap.test.ts:10-41](../../../frontend/src/lib/keyboard/publishedKeyMap.test.ts#L10)) must be updated together.

**Admin panel** — new Salespeople, Approvers, Approvals inbox, Counter profile editor, Pharmacist/restricted-drug pages; `DevicesPage` becomes `CountersPage`; live tiles re-keyed from users to counters.

**Printing/reports** — `salesman` print field becomes real ([companySettingsService.ts:387](../../../frontend/src/lib/settings/companySettingsService.ts#L387) → renderers in [printingEngine.ts](../../../frontend/src/lib/printing/printingEngine.ts), [dotMatrixRenderer.ts](../../../frontend/src/lib/printing/dotMatrixRenderer.ts)); pharmacist + registration line joins the clinical block gated by `showClinical` ([printingEngine.ts:868,1128-1129,1492-1493](../../../frontend/src/lib/printing/printingEngine.ts#L868)); new salesperson reports plus a **real** restricted-drug register replacing the current "all invoices" query.

**Sync/offline** — new queue entity types (`salesperson`, `approval`, `pharmacist_confirmation`) in [syncQueueService.ts:6-26](../../../frontend/src/lib/sync/syncQueueService.ts#L6); profile refresh rides heartbeat/SSE; offline cached credentials move from *users* to *approvers + PIN-holding salespeople + pharmacists* while staying in the encrypted vault ([offlineStaffVault.ts](../../../frontend/src/lib/auth/offlineStaffVault.ts)).

**Constitution** — [specs/constitution.md](../../constitution.md) currently says "Users, devices, counters — per existing IAM spec" ([line 50](../../constitution.md#L50)), i.e. this data is explicitly un-governed. The CR's decision 6 requires a new governed rule set: see [spec-delta.md §5](spec-delta.md).

**Test surface** — 12 existing feature suites encode today's behaviour and **will fail by design**: `IamAndDeviceRegistrationTest` (pairing-code approval, owner switch), `DeviceActivationSecurityTest` (store-key registration, owner login + TOTP), `RemoteCounterSyncTest`, `MultiCounterDeviceTest`, `PosStoreKeyCompatibilityTest` (local registry-key ↔ store-key compatibility), `StaffSessionPolicyTest` (one active counter per **user**), `PosStaffCrudStorePinTest` (branch staff CRUD), `LoggedInDevicesTest`, `CounterNameUniquenessTest`, `InvoiceApprovalTest`, `AdminParityTest`, `ComprehensiveQaBackendSuiteTest`; plus frontend `storeDeviceService.test.ts`, `staffAuthService*.test.tsx`, `remoteCounterSync.test.ts`, `multiCounterSimulation.test.ts`, `documentSequenceSync.test.ts`, `InvoiceApprovalDialog.test.tsx`, `invoiceApprovalService.dom.test.ts`.

## 6. Sequencing hazard (why order matters)

Part 1 must land before Part 2, and Part 2 before Part 3/5, because every later control hangs on an identity the till does not yet have. Two dependencies are hard:

- **The counter must be the tenant authority before PINs appear at the till.** Today the device token already forces a store ([AuthenticateDevice.php:12-100](../../../backend/app/Http/Middleware/AuthenticateDevice.php#L12)) — that is the right hook and should be extended rather than replaced.
- **Deleting staff login invalidates four consumers at once**: cash shifts, `counter_sessions`, approval "manager" mode (`approved_by_user_id` → `users`), and the offline hash cache. Each needs a replacement keyed on counter/approver before the login screen goes, or the till loses its money-reconciliation trail mid-release.
